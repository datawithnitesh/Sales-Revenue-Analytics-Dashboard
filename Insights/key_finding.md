## Key Insights

- The dashboard shows strong overall performance with approximately $29.36M in revenue, $12.08M in profit, and a healthy 41.1% profit margin.
- Bikes are the core revenue driver, contributing about 96.5% of total revenue and 95.2% of total profit.
- Accessories deliver the highest profit margin at around 62.6%, even though their revenue share is much smaller.
- Australia is the top-performing territory, generating about 30.9% of total revenue.
- Profit margins remain stable across major territories, mostly ranging between 40% and 42%.
- December is the strongest month, showing clear seasonal demand toward the end of the year.
- Revenue is distributed across many customers rather than depending on only a few large buyers.
- Customers with BikeBuyer = 1 account for nearly all revenue, showing that bike purchase intent is the most important customer segment.
